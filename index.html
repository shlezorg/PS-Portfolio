Profile

The Synergy of Logic and Creative Leadership

Raised with a deep appreciation for execution in Ambalangoda and currently operating out of Bandaragama, my professional identity thrives at the intersection of structural discipline and creative expression. As a full-stack engineer proficient in React, Android (Java), and C, I view software architecture as an intricate system that must be secure, transparent, and built with user experience in mind.

My technical foundation includes core networking configurations and hands-on vulnerability assessments—such as exploiting and mitigating Broken Object Level Authorization (BOLA) flaws. However, technical capacity is only one half of the equation. Serving as the President of our university's Art Circle, leading our Executive Board, and managing multi-tiered event productions has refined my real-world soft skills.

Additionally, my background as a musician and vocalist fuels my visual design capabilities, allowing me to bring an eye for detail, balance, and presentation to both corporate asset creation and digital interfaces.

Epilogue

Authentic Final Reflection

Reflecting on this academic year, the Professional Skills Development course has served as a pivotal pivot point. When I first entered the program, my mindset was almost entirely transactional—I measured my value by my compiler efficiency, system configurations, and capacity to build backend logic. I treated soft skills as something optional that belonged to non-technical disciplines.

However, balancing my studies with my role as President of the university Art Circle and coordinating multi-disciplinary student teams forced me to face my own limitations. I realized that the best system architecture will fail if the developers building it are unable to communicate, resolve conflict, or set healthy boundaries. Learning to observe my own behavior through the Johari Window, managing stress without "emotional leakage," and utilizing structured BATNA principles in group disputes transformed how I coordinate teams.

This portfolio represents more than just a course requirement; it compiles real evidence of my growth from a quiet, task-focused developer into a balanced, values-driven technical lead. I step forward into the corporate environment equipped not just with coding capacity, but with the empathy, self-awareness, and resilience necessary to build a sustainable, high-impact career.

Kavishka Shenal

Software Engineering Undergraduate & Art Circle President
